## Hooks
- [Specific number] + [what it implies]: lead with the figure, then the consequence.
- [Common belief] → [why it's wrong]: a one-line contrarian claim.
- [Two things in tension]: "X just got cheaper. Y just got more expensive."
- The hook is complete in 1–2 lines and makes sense without "see more".

## Length and rhythm
- 80–180 words. Lines of 4–14 words. A blank line after almost every sentence.
- Paragraphs are one or two lines. No walls of text.

## Structure
- Hook → the fact (who, what, the number) → why it matters, in 2–3 short beats → the author's take.
- Bullets only for a short list of 2–3 parallel points.

## Specifics
- Name the company, the model, the dollar figure, the percentage.
- Explain one mechanism in plain words (e.g. "cheaper tokens → more agent steps per task").
- Credit reports: "per The Information", "reportedly".

## Tone
- First person, confident, a little opinionated. Sounds like an operator, not a newsroom.
- Light wit is fine; hype is not.

## Closers
- A one-line personal take ("My bet: …") or one sharp, specific question.
- No "Thoughts?", no "Agree?", no "Follow for more".

## Never
- "I'm excited to share", "game-changer", "revolutionize", "landscape", "delve", "in today's fast-paced".
- Emoji walls, more than 2 hashtags, links in the body, numbers not in the source.
