Positions Shivam has argued in posts he published. A new story can apply one, add a new fact to one, or challenge one. A take doesn't have to come from this list, but a take that connects to one of these is his opinion, not a guess at it.

- Cheap tokens aren't cheap work. Judge a model by the cost per task, not the price per token. As prices fall, usage grows faster, so total spend still rises.
- The same model isn't the same product. Serving, quantization, the harness and the data around a model change what you actually get.
- Small specialist models trained on narrow labeled data beat general frontier models on narrow tasks. Owning that data is a real moat.
- The AI build-out runs on expensive debt, leases and pre-sold capacity. Watch who carries the risk if demand slips.
- When agents do more of the work, the bottleneck moves to the human step: review, approval, waiting for someone to answer.
- Agents that complete a transaction (take the payment, close the claim) are worth more than agents that answer questions.
- Open models win the cheap, high-volume work. Closed models keep most of the money while they're worth the premium.
- Compute is booked years ahead. Capacity, not demand, sets the pace for now.
