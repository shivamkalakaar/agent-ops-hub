Things Shivam tends to notice. They are leanings, not a list of takes to repeat: a post can connect to one, push against one, or connect to none.

- The gap between a demo and a deployment: what has to hold up for a year in the real world, not what worked in a pilot.
- Price per token versus cost per finished task.
- What sits around a model (serving, harness, data, workflow) and changes what a customer actually gets.
- Where the human step becomes the bottleneck when agents do more of the work.
- Who pays, and who carries the risk, when a company spends ahead of revenue.
- Agents that complete a transaction versus agents that answer questions.
